Six-Axis Stewart Platform

Built across four courses · Full Sail University · 2026

A servo-driven, six-degree-of-freedom motion platform carried through four consecutive courses: modeled and assembled digitally in SolidWorks first, then fabricated, wired, and programmed. Six micro servos on an ESP32 drive threaded pushrods with ball-link ends, moving a 3D-printed top plate through pitch, roll, yaw, heave, surge, and sway.

It runs in three modes: mirroring a handheld IMU in real time, playing scripted motion sequences (shown in the video), and following the player character in a Unity game so the platform moves with the gameplay.

The hardest part was fabrication tolerance. Servo pockets, standoff holes, and pin fits had to be dialed in across two processes, so I printed and laser-cut calibration plates first, measured the real clearances, and fed those numbers back into the SolidWorks models before cutting the final parts. The ESP32 sits on a custom milled copper PCB that breaks out all six servo headers.

Mixed Reality Accessibility Alerting System

Solo project · Full Sail University · July 2026

Real-time visual alerts for deaf and hard-of-hearing users, delivered in Meta Quest 3 passthrough. A single ESP32 running ESPHome carries an LD2410 mmWave radar, a temperature and humidity sensor, and an RGB LED. Home Assistant automations bridge those entities to the headset over MQTT, and an Unreal Engine 5.6 app renders the result in mixed reality.

Widgets pin to real walls and tables using Meta scene data and persist between sessions as spatial anchors: a climate panel with a live temperature ring, toggle cards for a lamp and the LED with a full color picker, and an ambient orb that pulses and blooms when the radar detects presence. Retained MQTT messages let the headset show current state the moment it launches.

Hardware debugging was half the work. I migrated the radar module to the ESP32 for 3.3V logic, tuned per-gate sensitivity to eliminate false alerts, and traced a silent boot failure to the LED being wired at power-on. The package ships with an APK installer, the full ESPHome config, and the five automations so anyone can reproduce it.

JustInTime Trainer

Team project · XR Systems Developer · Full Sail University · June 2026

A proof-of-concept AR guide for nurses operating hospital and clinic IV machines. The headset overlays step-by-step guidance on the machine and pairs it with an assistant that answers questions at the bedside, showing how just-in-time help could cut setup errors and training time.

Built in Unreal Engine for the Meta Quest 3 as the development target, with smart glasses as the intended end product. Demoed live to industry reviewers, with the headset view mirrored to the room.
